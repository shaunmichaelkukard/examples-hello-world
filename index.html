Hello, I'm Tamsin

Baking that leaves nobody out

The Self Raising Baker started the way most good bakeries do — with someone we love who couldn't eat the cake at the party. So we learned to bake without gluten, without dairy and without eggs, and refused to accept that "free-from" should ever mean "second best".

Everything is mixed, baked and finished to order in our Johannesburg kitchen. Tell us what you can't eat — and what you're celebrating — and we'll build the bake around it.

Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Are you a completely nut-free kitchen?

We bake nut-free by default and can guarantee a nut-free batch when you tell us upfront. Because we handle nuts in some recipes, always flag a nut allergy on your order so we can schedule your bake accordingly.

What exactly is free from?

Gluten, dairy and egg are kept out of every bake on the menu. Nut free, soy free, sesame free, vegan and no-added-sugar options are available on request — use the filter on the menu, or tell us in the order form.

Do you do diabetic friendly and sugar-free?

Yes. Most items can be made sugar-reduced or sugar-free using alternatives. Tell us which sweeteners you're comfortable with and we'll work to that.

How much notice do you need?

Around 48 hours for most orders. Celebration, novelty and wedding cakes should be booked as early as you can — a few weeks is ideal for anything sculpted or tiered.

Do you deliver?

Collection in Johannesburg, with local delivery arranged when you order. Send us your suburb and we'll quote the delivery with the bake.
